# 🏥 Hospital Operations & Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Hospital Operations & Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) Analysis** is an interactive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze hospital financial performance, patient admissions, insurance claims, profitability, and revenue cycle operations.
---
The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key hospital KPIs and helps identify department-level profitability, insurance claim outcomes, patient admission patterns, monthly financial performance, and payment sources.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall hospital revenue and profitability
- Monitor patient admission volume and admission types
- Analyze insurance claim approval, denial, and review status
- Identify the most profitable hospital departments
- Track monthly financial performance
- Compare insurance payouts with out-of-pocket payments
- Monitor claim denial rates and revenue cycle performance
- Provide actionable insights for hospital management

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Department-wise Profit

Analyzes profitability across major hospital departments, including:

- Cardiology
- Orthopedics
- Emergency
- Oncology
- Neurology
- General Surgery

This helps identify high-performing departments and understand their contribution to overall hospital profitability.

### 2. Insurance Claim Status

Visualizes insurance claim outcomes:

- Approved
- Denied
- Under Review

This analysis helps monitor the efficiency of the insurance revenue cycle and identify potential areas contributing to claim denials.

### 3. Patient Volume by Admission Type

Analyzes patient admissions based on:

- Elective
- Emergency
- Urgent

This provides insights into patient flow and hospital resource requirements.

### 4. Monthly Financial Performance

Tracks monthly financial performance throughout the year to identify:

- Revenue trends
- High-performing months
- Low-performing months
- Financial fluctuations

### 5. Insurance Payout vs Out-of-Pocket Payment

Compares payment sources across hospital departments:

- Insurance Payout
- Patient Out-of-Pocket Payment
- Total Payment

This helps understand the contribution of insurance and direct patient payments to hospital revenue.

## 💡 Key Insights

- Cardiology is one of the highest-profit departments in the hospital.
- Insurance claims are predominantly approved, while denied and under-review claims represent opportunities for revenue cycle improvement.
- Elective admissions contribute the largest share of patient volume.
- Hospital financial performance varies across months, highlighting opportunities for financial planning.
- Insurance payouts represent a significant component of total patient payments.
- Department-level payment analysis can help management identify revenue concentration and optimize financial performance.
